### Venk Murthy, MD, PhD

Cardiologist and physician-scientist at the University of Michigan. My lab builds quantitative
and AI methods that pull diagnostic information out of cardiac imaging, the ECG, and multiomic
data, to find disease that standard tests miss.

- Melvyn Rubenfire Professor of Preventive Cardiology · Professor of Internal Medicine and Radiology
- Associate Chief of Cardiology for Translational Research and Innovation · Director of Nuclear Cardiology
- Management Committee, [Michigan Institute for Data & AI in Society](https://midas.umich.edu/)
- Member, U.S. Preventive Services Task Force<br>
  <sub><i>The views expressed in here are solely those of the author and do not necessarily represent the views of the USPSTF or the Federal Government.</i></sub>

I speak and teach widely on AI in medicine — helping clinicians understand what these tools can
and can't do — through grand rounds, national society meetings, and professional working groups.

#### What we work on

- **Coronary vasomotor and microvascular dysfunction:** quantitative cardiac PET, myocardial blood flow, and flow reserve
- **Multiomic biomarkers:** metabolomic and proteomic signatures of cardiometabolic disease in large longitudinal cohorts
- **AI for imaging and risk:** foundation models and deep learning that extend quantitative capability to widely available tests like the ECG

Most of our code lives at **[@vlmurthylab](https://github.com/vlmurthylab)**. Interested in joining? See [murthylab.org/join](https://murthylab.org/join.html).
